# Accessing the contributors graph (corrected step 4, proposed to github/docs)

## Step 2: Under your repository name, click Insights

Under your repository name, click Insights.
Verify that the repository's Insights page is visible.

## Step 3: In the left sidebar, click Contributors

In the left sidebar, click Contributors.
Verify that the contributors graph for the repository is visible.

## Step 4: Optionally, to view contributors during a specific time period, to the right of "Contributors," click the Period button (for example, Period: Last 3 months). Then select a time period

Optionally, to view contributors during a specific time period, to the right of "Contributors," click the Period button (for example, Period: Last 3 months). Then select a time period.
Verify that a menu for choosing a time period is open.
