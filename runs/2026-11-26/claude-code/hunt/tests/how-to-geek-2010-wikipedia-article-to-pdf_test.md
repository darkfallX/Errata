# How-To Geek (2010): Wikipedia article to PDF

## Step 1: To do this, open the Tools menu at the top of the article in Wikipedia when you're on an article you want to save

To do this, open the Tools menu at the top of the article in Wikipedia when you're on an article you want to save.
Verify that the article's print and export options are shown.

## Step 2: Click Download as PDF to have Wikipedia automatically generate a PDF copy of this article

Click Download as PDF to have Wikipedia automatically generate a PDF copy of this article.
Verify that a page offering to download the article as a PDF is shown.
