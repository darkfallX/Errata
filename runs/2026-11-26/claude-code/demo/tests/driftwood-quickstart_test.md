# Driftwood quickstart

## Step 1: Open the Driftwood dashboard and click Projects

Open the Driftwood dashboard and click Projects.
Verify that the Projects list is visible.

## Step 2: Click Developers → Tokens and press New token

Go to http://localhost:4177.
Click Developers → Tokens and press New token.
Verify that a form to create a new API key or token is visible.

## Step 4: Open Team and click Invite member

Go to http://localhost:4177.
Open Team and click Invite member.
Verify that a form to invite a new member by email is visible.

## Step 5: Go to Account → Billing history

Go to http://localhost:4177.
Go to Account → Billing history.
Verify that a list of past invoices with dates and amounts is visible.
