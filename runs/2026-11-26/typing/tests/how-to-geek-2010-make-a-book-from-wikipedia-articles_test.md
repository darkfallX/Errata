# How-To Geek (2010): Make a book from Wikipedia articles

## Step 1: To do this, type Special:Book into Search in Wikipedia and press Enter to open the Book creator

To do this, type Special:Book into Search in Wikipedia and press Enter to open the Book creator.
Verify that wikipedia's Book creator page is shown.

## Step 2: Open Tools, then open the Main menu and click Special pages, then Book; click Wikipedia The Free Encyclopedia to get started

Open Tools, then open the Main menu and click Special pages, then Book; click Wikipedia The Free Encyclopedia to get started.
Verify that the book creator is on, offering to add the current page to a book.
