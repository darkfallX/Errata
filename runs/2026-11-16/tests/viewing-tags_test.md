# Viewing tags

## Step 2: To the right of the list of files, click Releases

To the right of the list of files, click Releases.
Verify that the repository's Releases page is visible.

## Step 3: At the top of the page, click Tags

At the top of the page, click Tags.
Verify that a list of the repository's tags is visible.
