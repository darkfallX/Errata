# Accessing the forks page

## Step 2: Under your repository name, click Insights

Under your repository name, click Insights.
Verify that the repository's Insights page is visible.

## Step 3: In the left sidebar, click Forks

In the left sidebar, click Forks.
Verify that a list of the repository's forks is visible.

## Step 4: Optionally, to sort the list by most starred forks, most recently updated forks, most open issues, or most open pull requests, click Sort, then choose an option from the dropdown menu

Optionally, to sort the list by most starred forks, most recently updated forks, most open issues, or most open pull requests, click Sort, then choose an option from the dropdown menu.
Verify that a menu of sort options for the forks list is open.
