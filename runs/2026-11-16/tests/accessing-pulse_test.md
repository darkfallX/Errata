# Accessing Pulse

## Step 2: Under your repository name, click Insights

Under your repository name, click Insights.
Verify that the Pulse overview of recent repository activity is visible.

## Step 3: Optionally, to choose a different time period, select the Period dropdown menu in the upper-right corner of the Pulse overview

Optionally, to choose a different time period, select the Period dropdown menu in the upper-right corner of the Pulse overview.
Verify that a menu for choosing the Pulse time period is open.
