# Help:Permanent link

## Step 1: A permalink can be made by clicking 'Permanent link' in the sidebar's toolbox or by accessing a prior revision of a page from its page history tab

A permalink can be made by clicking 'Permanent link' in the sidebar's toolbox or by accessing a prior revision of a page from its page history tab.
Verify that the page is shown at a permanent link to its current revision (the address includes oldid).
