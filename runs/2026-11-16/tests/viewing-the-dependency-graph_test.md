# Viewing the dependency graph

## Step 2: Under your repository name, click Insights

Under your repository name, click Insights.
Verify that the repository's Insights page is visible.

## Step 3: In the left sidebar, click Dependency graph

In the left sidebar, click Dependency graph.
Verify that the repository's dependency graph, listing its dependencies, is visible.

## Step 4: Optionally, to view the repositories and packages that depend on your repository, under "Dependency graph", click Dependents

Optionally, to view the repositories and packages that depend on your repository, under "Dependency graph", click Dependents.
Verify that a list of repositories and packages that depend on this repository is visible.
