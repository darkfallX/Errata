# Dignited (2019): Export a Wikipedia page as a PDF

## Step 1: Click Tools

Click Tools.
Verify that the article's print and export options are shown.

## Step 2: Select Download as PDF from the list

Select Download as PDF from the list.
Verify that a page offering to download the article as a PDF is shown.
