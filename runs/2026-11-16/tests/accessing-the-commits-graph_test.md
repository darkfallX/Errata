# Accessing the commits graph

## Step 2: Under your repository name, click Insights

Under your repository name, click Insights.
Verify that the repository's Insights page is visible.

## Step 3: In the left sidebar, click Commits

In the left sidebar, click Commits.
Verify that a graph of commits to the repository over time is visible.
