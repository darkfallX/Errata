# Accessing the network graph

## Step 2: Under your repository name, click Insights

Under your repository name, click Insights.
Verify that the repository's Insights page is visible.

## Step 3: In the left sidebar, click Network

In the left sidebar, click Network.
Verify that the repository's network graph of branches and forks is visible.
