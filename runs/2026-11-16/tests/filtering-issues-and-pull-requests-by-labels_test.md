# Filtering issues and pull requests by labels

## Step 2: Under your repository name, click Issues or Pull requests

Under your repository name, click Issues or Pull requests.
Verify that the repository's list of issues is visible.

## Step 3: Above the list of issues or pull requests, click Labels

Above the list of issues or pull requests, click Labels.
Verify that the repository's list of labels is visible.
