# Vancouver Editor (2011): How to cite a Wikipedia page

## Step 1: Look for "Tools" on the left side of the page and click on "Cite this page."

Look for "Tools" on the left side of the page and click on "Cite this page."
Verify that a page with ready-made citations for the article is shown.
