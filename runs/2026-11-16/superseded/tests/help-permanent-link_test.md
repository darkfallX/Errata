# Help:Permanent link

## Step 1: A permalink can be made by clicking "Permanent link" in the sidebar's toolbox

A permalink can be made by clicking "Permanent link" in the sidebar's toolbox.
Verify that the page is shown at a permanent link to its current revision (the address includes oldid).
