# How-To Geek (2010): Make a book from Wikipedia articles

## Step 1: Open Search → Tools → Search → Main → Special pages and click Book

Open Search → Tools → Search → Main → Special pages and click Book.
Verify that wikipedia's Book creator page is shown.

## Step 2: Open Suggest pages and click Wikipedia The Free Encyclopedia

Open Suggest pages and click Wikipedia The Free Encyclopedia.
Verify that the book creator is on, offering to add the current page to a book.
