# labnol (2015): Create eBooks from Wikipedia

## Step 1: Open any article and pick "Book Creator" from the toolbox menu in the right sidebar

Open any article and pick "Book Creator" from the toolbox menu in the right sidebar.
Verify that wikipedia's book creator is on for this article.

## Step 2: Click the "Add this page to your book" link

Click the "Add this page to your book" link.
Verify that the current article is added to a book.
